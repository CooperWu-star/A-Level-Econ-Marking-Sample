## Key differences vs A-Level when building IGCSE slides

- **Vocabulary** is lighter. Avoid words like "ceteris paribus", "Pareto", "MES", "monopsony" unless you explicitly gloss them.
- **No A2 material.** Do not include indifference curves, cost-curve algebra, kinked-demand MR gaps, or Pareto optimality.
- **Diagrams are simpler.** Stick to: D/S, shifts in D/S, PPC, AD/AS (basic), circular flow (2-sector OK), tariff (no welfare-triangle decomposition).
- **Cases should be globally accessible.** Lean on widely-known events (COVID inflation, oil shocks, big-tech) — IGCSE markets are global.
- **Slide count per chapter is usually smaller** — aim 10–18 slides rather than 20–30.
