# Coursebook map — syllabus topic ↔ textbook chapter

Maps every **9708 syllabus topic** to its chapter in the endorsed coursebook so
a deck follows the **textbook's teaching order** and can cite the chapter the
class is reading.

> **Textbook:** *Cambridge International AS & A Level Economics Coursebook*,
> Colin Bamford & Susan Grant (Cambridge University Press), endorsed for 9708.
> Structure: **11 units → 54 chapters**. The book is laid out as **one chapter
> per syllabus topic, in syllabus order** — so chapter *N* and topic *X.Y* line
> up exactly. Chapter 54 ("Preparing for assessment") has no topic.

## How to use this in a deck

1. After you fix the course + topic (SKILL.md Step 1–2), look the topic up here
   to get its **unit + chapter**.
2. Put the chapter in the deck's `kicker` (e.g. `9708 · 2.1 · Coursebook Unit 2, Ch 7`)
   and in the title slide subtitle, so students can read along.
3. Follow the **chapter's internal section order** (`X.1, X.2 …` mirror the
   syllabus sub-points) when sequencing slides — that is the order the textbook
   and the class use.
4. The **Diagrams** column lists the `diagram_id`s from `diagram-catalog.md`
   that fit that topic. `—` means no library diagram yet → use a `content`
   slide and leave a `TODO_DIAGRAM:` note (see diagram-catalog.md).
